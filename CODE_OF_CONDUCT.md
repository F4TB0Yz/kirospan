# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in KiroSpan to be safe, respectful, and constructive.
Contributors and maintainers must:

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks or harassment;
- respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints;
- accept technical feedback in good faith;
- avoid publishing another person's private information or credentials;
- keep security reports and sensitive evidence in private channels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and retaliation against a
person who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement decisions
should be proportional, documented privately, and applied consistently.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's private reporting
channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include credentials or
private user data. Maintainers will acknowledge the report, review available
evidence, and protect the reporter's privacy as far as reasonably possible.
